JobPilot: Daily 09:00 AM Curation & 1-Tap Apply

A job seeker opens the 09:00 AM Telegram job card, optionally previews a zero-hallucination LaTeX resume, and taps 1-Tap Apply for off-screen ShadowMux submission.

Shoal rehearsal · 5,000 synthetic users · seed 7 · 24 replications · generated 2026-11-13 12:06

What the rehearsal says

  • Out of 100 eligible users, about 4 are predicted to finish (range 3–6%).
  • The biggest loss is at “Reads daily Telegram match card”: 32% of everyone who started leaves there, mostly because they could not proceed (blocked).
  • 65% of eligible users never see the feature at all. Discovery, not the flow, caps adoption.
  • Best single fix: Give users without shadowmux_online a way through 'Acquires ShadowMux desktop lease & cookies' (+3.0 points of adoption).
  • 18 high-severity edge cases are not covered by the spec (18 confirmed in review).
  • Hypothesis H1 looks wrong: “Daily Engagement: at least 85% of eligible candidates open and review the 09:00 AM Telegram card.” (predicted 36%).
  • Hypothesis H2 looks wrong: “Application Confirmation Rate: at least 40% of candidates who open a card finish applying.” (predicted 12%).
  • Hypothesis H3 looks wrong: “Execution Cycle Time: median time from opening the card to stamped receipt is 45 seconds or less.” (predicted 73s).

Gaps in the spec

  • high Desktop ShadowMux offline when user taps 1-Tap Apply on mobile: The proposal pushes Telegram cards at 09:00 AM (mobile commute time) but assumes desktop Chrome/ShadowMux is awake and reachable.
  • high What happens after the 180-second HITL CAPTCHA/question timeout: User Story 8 specifies waiting up to 180s for human resolution, but never defines whether state is saved, retried, or discarded after timeout.
  • high Ambiguous outcome if confirmation-page scraping fails after clicking Submit: If network drops or ATS DOM changes after clicking Submit, the spec does not say how JobPilot verifies whether the application went through without double-submitting.
  • med Compiler fallback returns .tex source when tectonic/pdflatex is absent: In apps/jobpilot/compiler.py, missing TeX binaries assign out_tex_path to result.pdf_path, which would fail or send raw LaTeX on Preview PDF.

How to read this, and its limits

  • These are predictions from a model of your feature, not measurements of real people. Treat every number as a hypothesis to check.
  • The ranges cover random variation and a ±10% wobble on the guessed inputs. They do not cover a wrong model (a missing step, a missing kind of user).
  • Language models asked to play users tend to be more agreeable, more goal-directed and less varied than real people. Qualitative sessions find problems; they do not prove their absence.
  • Rank order (which step is worst, which fix helps most) is more reliable than absolute levels. Calibrate with real data when you have any, and score the run after launch.

Method: each synthetic user gets traits (motivation, skill, patience, trust), a situation and capabilities drawn from their segment, then walks the flow. At each step the chance of carrying on rises with motivation, visible value and trust and falls with effort, unclear screens, errors and fatigue. All formulas are in shoal/sim.py; the model used is saved next to this report as model.json.
